## Positioning

WeTransfer competes on a low-friction, visually polished delivery experience and broad familiarity. Its current product surface goes beyond a link: email delivery, previews, file requests, post-send controls, access restrictions, branding, mobile apps, comments, team workspaces, and enterprise administration all reduce workflow friction.

## Strengths

- Familiar, polished recipient experience across web and native mobile apps.
- Mature delivery workflows: email sends, previews, file requests, comments, branding, recovery, and post-send management.
- Free passwords and recipient access restrictions.
- 1 TB published top transfer size plus enterprise administration and ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
- Strong first-party scale signals: 30M+ monthly transfers and 12M+ monthly active users.

## Weaknesses Relative to anon.li Drop

- Sending requires an account, while anon.li supports a limited anonymous guest flow.
- Official security documentation describes provider-managed encryption, not client-side zero-knowledge encryption.
- The free rolling limit is 3 GB and 10 transfers versus anon.li's account bandwidth model.
- Folder uploads are documented as flattening hierarchy unless the sender pre-zips; anon.li now preserves relative folder paths.
- The product does not combine file delivery with privacy aliases, an open-source web app, CLI/API/MCP workflows, or user-held Drop keys.

## Competitive Implications for anon.li

anon.li cannot win merely by offering bigger free numbers. It should win trust-sensitive transfers through provider-blind encryption, anonymous sending, transparent open cryptography, exact access controls, folder fidelity, and automation—while matching WeTransfer's basics: dependable large transfers, previews, recipient delivery, requests, mobile ergonomics, post-send editing, and obvious recovery paths.

The clearest honest message is: **WeTransfer is easier and more mature for general creative delivery; anon.li is designed for transfers the service provider itself should be unable to read.**
