# WeTransfer plan limits — research notes

Source: https://wetransfer.com/help-center/subscriptions/plan-limits  
Pulled: 2026-07-13  
Source page updated: 2026-05-30

Paraphrased observations:

- Free: at most 10 transfers or 3 GB total in a rolling 30-day window.
- Starter: at most 10 transfers or 300 GB total in a rolling 30-day window.
- Ultimate: unlimited transfer count, with a published maximum of 1 TB per transfer.
- Ultimate includes custom expiry, passwords, branded pages, and access control.
- Teams adds shared workspaces, team branding controls, and administration.
- Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM, custom storage, and compliance features.
- The source says free and paid subscriptions receive the same transfer speeds.

No price was visible on this server-rendered help page. Pricing can vary by locale and promotion and should be checked separately.
