# WeTransfer free-plan product page — research notes

Source: https://wetransfer.com/resources/free-file-transfer/wetransfer-free-plan  
Pulled: 2026-07-13

Paraphrased observations:

- Sending now requires a free account; recipients can download without an account.
- Free users can share by link or email, with up to 10 email recipients per transfer.
- Password protection and restriction to named email addresses are available on the free plan.
- Free links expire after at most 3 days.
- The page advertises previews for images, video, audio, and PDF files.
- WeTransfer reports more than 30 million monthly transfers, 12 million monthly active users, and a 4.8 average mobile-store rating. These are first-party marketing claims, not independently verified metrics.
- The product emphasizes a familiar, install-free recipient experience, no compression, creative workflows, customization, and collaboration.
