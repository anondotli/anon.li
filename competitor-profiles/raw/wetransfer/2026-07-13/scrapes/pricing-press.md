# WeTransfer pricing announcement — research notes

Source: https://pressage.wetransfer.com/blog/story/2025-wetransfer-plans-remix-see-our-latest-plans

Paraphrased observations:

Fresh checkout pricing was not accessible in the research environment. Treat USD 6.99 as the last observed official announcement price, not a guaranteed current price for every locale.
