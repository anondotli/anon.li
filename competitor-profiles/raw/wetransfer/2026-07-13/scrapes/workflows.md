# WeTransfer workflow capabilities — research notes

Sources:

- https://wetransfer.com/help-center/how-to/send-a-transfer
- https://wetransfer.com/help-center/how-to/track-file-requests

Pulled: 2026-07-13  
Source pages updated: 2026-05-30 and 2026-06-24

Paraphrased observations:

- Senders can choose email delivery or a shareable link and can use desktop, mobile web, iOS, or Android.
- Free email transfers support 10 recipients; paid plans support 50.
- Folder upload is supported, but the documented default behavior flattens folder contents into one ZIP; users are told to pre-ZIP when preserving hierarchy matters.
- File requests can be copied, closed, deleted, and managed from a received-transfer area.
- Requested transfers expire after 3 days on Free/Starter and 30 days on Ultimate/Teams.
- The workflow includes recipient email notifications and download confirmation.
